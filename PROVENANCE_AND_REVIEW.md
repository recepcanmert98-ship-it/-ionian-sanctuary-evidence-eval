# Methodological provenance and source audit

**Project origin:** The present study derives its analytical themes (coin circulation, provenance, attribution, source independence, archaeological inference) from the author's Ionian sanctuary research and the frozen V4/C8 agent archive. The original archive supplied by the author also contains **constructed/synthetic** accession and coin-plate examples. Those constructed accession IDs were **not** transferred into the real-source gold answers. They must not be presented as registered Claros archaeological objects.

**Documentary evidence:** Eight cases draw from real public Roman Provincial Coinage entries (I 2521–2525), Zeynep Çizmeli Öğün's study of Claros coins, and the Notion Archaeological Project's online report history. These are short curated assertions with traceable URLs, not reproductions of original protected catalogue coin photographs. Specific catalogue readings and counts are frozen in `tasks/` and `sources/source_manifest.json`.

**Photographs:** The four Commons exhibits and their authors, credits and licenses are recorded in `PHOTOGRAPH_CREDITS.md`. No comparison image is claimed to depict one of the RPC I 2521–2525 specimens. An Aurelian coin with a British findspot is explicitly a *comparative object*.

**Independent review required:** A professional archaeologist/numismatist should confirm the eight reference answer objects and the assertions in the frozen prompts, particularly distinguishable specimen counts, Greek legend transcription, the Metropolis attribution, and non-equivalence of Apollo iconography and excavation provenance. Tests only validate software mechanics.

**Research integrity:** Neither a positive-control oracle nor a negative-control string is a frontier-system outcome. Actual external trial collection has not occurred within this package. It is not appropriate to fill in observed failure rates or to invent session screenshots, scores, or citations. Tested-system identity and version metadata must remain accessible to judges even when presentation branding is suppressed.

**Software assistance disclosure:** This build's Python, HTML, and documentation were prepared with assistance from a language-based drafting tool, and must be reviewed by the submitting researcher. That assistance does not establish the truth of the archaeological answers, nor does it substitute for collecting true experimental outcomes. Include this disclosure in the final competition submission even if vendor branding is removed from the presentation screens.
