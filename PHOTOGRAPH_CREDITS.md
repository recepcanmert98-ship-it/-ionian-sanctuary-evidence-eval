### Local photo files

Run `GET_PHOTOS_WINDOWS.cmd` (or `python fetch_photos.py`) once to retrieve the original JPEG bytes from Wikimedia into the `photos/` folder. The downloader records SHA-256 digests after saving. There is no password, account, or commercial image library dependency.

The initial ZIP itself provides full metadata, filenames, download code, gallery, and source URLs. It does not contain the large real photograph binaries until the downloader has been executed; thereafter the photographs are available as local files for offline viewing, archival copies, and submission under the relevant licenses.
